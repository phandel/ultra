04 Why these

The limiter is mechanical durability, not aerobic capacity.

Heart rate held flat at 122 bpm across 10.4 hours on the 40-miler with 6.3% decoupling — the engine is built. What breaks down is the chassis: cadence 156 spm (elite 170–180), ground contact 294 ms (elite <250), vertical ratio 10.3% (efficient <7).

Steep descents train exactly that. Eccentric quad loading is what keeps the legs from failing at mile 35 on 9/5, and it's hard to find in San Jose. Here it's a quarter mile from the door.

Hiking costs about 5% of running-specific fitness in a week — mostly pace familiarity, not endurance — and buys time on feet with the loading pattern that matters. The one thing it doesn't train is cadence, so keep the short cadence-drill runs.
Two caveats. These links use Google's walking mode, so it snaps between waypoints with its own routing — exact on pavement, but its trail data at the reserve is thin and may draw odd connectors. The GPX files are the precise geometry; use those on the watch. And all mile markers count from the Elkhorn Rd junction — offset by wherever the driveway sits.
